Walkizz Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Walkizz ("the game") is made by Libracap Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17568063). This policy explains what happens to information when you or your child play Walkizz. We wrote it to be simple, because the game is made for families.

The short version

Walkizz does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Game progress is saved only on your own device.

What is stored, and where

To remember your progress, the game saves things like the walker names you type in, the dogs and outfits you have unlocked, your bones and your trophies. This is saved only on your phone or tablet. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the game deletes this information.

Walker names are just nicknames for the game. Please don't use a child's full real name.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos or location.
  • We do not show adverts or use advertising IDs.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell or share any information with anyone.
  • The game works offline and does not need an internet connection to play.

In-app purchases

Some extra dogs and outfits can be bought inside the game. Purchases are behind a grown-up check so children cannot buy by accident. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store, under their own privacy policies. We never see or receive your payment details. If we use a purchase-checking service to confirm that a purchase is genuine, it receives only an anonymous purchase record from the store, never your name or payment details.

Children

Walkizz is designed to be suitable for children. Because we do not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect any information from children. This policy is written to meet the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, UK GDPR, the EU GDPR and the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Walkizz adds features that use information differently, for example online play, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date at the top.
